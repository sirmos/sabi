      How Sabi decides

      Sabi never says a product is genuine. It combines three things: warning signs on the pack, whether NAFDAC has a record for the number, and whether that record fits the pack (name, product type, manufacturer, expiry). No record is a strong red flag. A matching record with a clean pack means “looks consistent”.
